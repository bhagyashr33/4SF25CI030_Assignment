Disney is a global entertainment company built around strong intellectual property and storytelling. 
Its business spans animation, film, television, streaming, theme parks, licensing, and merchandise. 
A major strength of Disney is its ability to transform successful characters and stories into multiple revenue-generating products and experiences. 
The company also uses technology, digital platforms, and audience data to adapt to changing consumer preferences. 
Its theme parks combine entertainment, technology, and immersive storytelling to create unique experiences. 
Disney’s growth demonstrates how creativity, branding, innovation, and diversification can work together to build a powerful global entertainment ecosystem.
