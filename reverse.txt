
Formula 1, or F1, is one of the most exciting motorsports in the world. 
It features skilled drivers racing high-performance cars on specially designed circuits and street tracks. 
F1 races require speed, concentration, teamwork, and careful strategy. Drivers must manage their tyres, fuel, and racing position throughout the event. 
Pit stops are also important, as teams quickly change tyres and make adjustments to the car. 
Engineers use data and advanced technology to improve performance and make important decisions during races.
 With famous events like the Monaco Grand Prix, F1 attracts millions of fans worldwide. It represents the perfect combination of technology, teamwork, and driving skill.
